**The trust vocabulary — four states, and the distinctions ARE the product:**

| State | Means |
|---|---|
| `MEASURED` | A named check ran and passed. Traceable to that check. |
| `APPROXIMATE` | Measured against a documented proxy. Always carries its caveat. |
| `NOT_CHECKED` | Nobody looked. **Not** a warning, **not** a failure — an absence. |
| `FAILED` | A check ran and did not pass. |

**Exit codes:** `0` VERIFIED, `2` NOT_CHECKED, anything else FAILED. A gate that goes red
for environmental reasons gets ignored within a week, at which point it is worse than no
gate — so distinguish "could not look" from "looked and failed" in every predicate you
write.

### 5. Cross-family critique requirements

### 4. HITL Relaxation Ladders (tied to grants and RepID tier)

**Canonical constants:** `REPID_HITL_GATE = 70`, `CONFIDENCE_GATE = 0.8`.

**Ladder (rungs unlock only on MEASURED evidence; APPROXIMATE values block ascent):**

- **Rung 0 (baseline — all agents):** Full HITL review on every high-stakes action (spend > 10 DP, tier promotion to AUTONOMOUS+, grant minting). Default.
- **Rung 1 (EARNING or G6 holder, RepID ≥ 70, measured confidence ≥ 0.8):** Reduce to sampled review (20% of actions). Requires MEASURED tier (database-derived, never app-written) and MEASURED confidence (named check, not proxy). **A_eff is APPROXIMATE and can overstate**; any rung consuming it must record `authorityIsApproximate: true` and cap relaxation at sampled review only.
- **Rung 2 (AUTONOMOUS+ or G6 + measured counterparties ≥ 2, RepID ≥ 1000, measured confidence ≥ 0.9):** Reduce to exception-only review (human only on anomaly). Same MEASURED requirements. Descent trigger below.
- **Rung 3 (VETERAN, measured counterparties ≥ 2, measured cross-family critique on last 5 claims):** No routine HITL (audit log only). Requires MEASURED evidence only.

**Descent rules (ratchet, not one-way):**  
- Any `FAILED` on a measured check (including zk proof rejection or constitutional audit if ever enabled).  
- Tier downgrade (trigger overwrites tier on next RepID move; counterparty count lag also demotes).  
- `A_eff` outcome changes to `NOT_CHECKED` or bindingTerm becomes builder_floor.  
- Loss of G6.  
- Any single measured confidence < 0.8.

**Gate:** `isHITLRequired(action, agentState): boolean` implements the ladder. Consuming `A_eff` always emits the approximation warning.

**Rollback:** Revert to full HITL on all rungs (single constant change to REPID_HITL_GATE=100 or equivalent).

**Fail-open vector & prevention:** APPROXIMATE A_eff overstates authority → premature rung ascent. Prevented by explicit `authorityIsApproximate` stamp (see effective-authority.ts) that blocks Rung 2+ and forces the caveat in every log. Absent confidence field treated as < 0.8 (`NOT_CHECKED` → full HITL). Matches bounty-authorization.ts "looks-correct-but-authorises-everyone" prevention.

### 5. Cross-Family Critique Requirements

**Observable property (no vendor names):** A claim has received *cross-family critique* when it has been reviewed by at least one prover whose model-family identifier (observable via `modelFamily` or equivalent registry field) differs from the claimant's primary family, **and** the reviewer’s output contains an independent confidence score and a signed critique artifact.

**When required:** For any claim reaching Rung 2 or 3 of the HITL ladder, or any claim used in zk selective disclosure, or any claim whose confidence contributes to `A_eff` calculation.

**Predicate `hasCrossFamilyCritique(claim): CritiqueDecision`**

- No critique artifact present: `NOT_CHECKED`.
- Critique present but same-family: `NOT_CHECKED` (insufficient diversity).
- Valid different-family critique with confidence ≥ CONFIDENCE_GATE and matching signed artifact: `MEASURED`.
- Critique present but confidence < gate or artifact invalid: `FAILED`.

Prevented by requiring observable `modelFamily` mismatch in the registry (registry-first discipline from cost-class.ts). Absent critique field = NOT_CHECKED (closed). No vendor names used.
