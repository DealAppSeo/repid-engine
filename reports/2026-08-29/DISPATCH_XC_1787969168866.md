### Why this task exists

The product is about to grow a **progressive-trust signup**: a visitor can arrive with no
wallet, no email and no account, do real things, and see their RepID move — proving more
about themselves only as the stakes rise. That is the MVP's front door and it is not built
yet. **You are being asked BEFORE it is built, not after**, because the failure direction
here is "anyone can mint reputation", and that is far cheaper to prevent than to unwind.

The operator's stated intent, verbatim, because it is not written anywhere in this repo:

> *"we want to create the signup with the least friction possible … for most users we want
> them to see value before we ask for too much info … without us ever taking seeing or
> having access to their info or wallet, we never take custody. So as they do a few things
> and learn, see the value hopefully, and their RepID goes up slightly because they are
> learning … even though at a certain point they obviously will have to 2FA and I see
> value in 4FA before they start exchanging high risk data."*

Your job is to find where that intent, implemented naively, breaks the guarantee that a
RepID is **earned**.

**2. Rank the two RepID options by failure direction**

- **(A) Provisional-and-vesting**: Anonymous actions move a visible RepID on the token-only `builders` row (`current_repid` updated via `recomputeBuilderRepID` or scoring paths). On identity binding (ERC-7231 mint / full-account upgrade), the score "vests" (transfers to a real `repid_agents` row or becomes `earns_repid_rewards=true`).
  - **Attacker gains**: Farm many token-only rows, perform low-friction actions (demo rounds, simulated stakes, marketplace posts) to inflate builder RepID/mean-active scores, then bind a small number of them to real identities/wallets. Accumulates "earned" reputation that survives binding. The existing counterparty gate (`compute_tier(integer, uuid)` overload in the Postgres trigger, demoting VETERAN/AUTONOMOUS without >=2 unique counterparties) **covers (A) only partially** — it stops helping at ESTABLISHED (the floor for most agents today; EARNING/PROBATIONARY have no gate). A farm of zero-counterparty token-only accounts can push builder RepID into ESTABLISHED (and authority) before binding.
  - **Honest user loses**: Delayed vesting friction; must re-prove actions or lose provisional progress if binding fails.
  - **Failure direction when subtly wrong**: Fails **open** (Sybil farm accrues persistent value that vests; the "provisional" flag is easy to forget in binding logic, as seen in the bounty-authorization.ts precedent where `admin` scope was granted to all). Subtle error (e.g., binding copies `current_repid` without clearing provisional state or without counterparty proof) turns the entire progressive signup into a reputation mint.

- **(B) Preview-only**: The displayed number is computed on-the-fly (e.g., via demo snapshot or ephemeral score) but **never persisted** to `builders.current_repid`, `repid_score_events`, or any `repid_agents` row for token-only accounts. Binding starts from baseline (STARTING_REPID).
  - **Attacker gains**: Nothing persistent. Can spam previews but no ledger trail, no authority boost, no vesting.
  - **Honest user loses**: No visible progress during learning/onboarding ("RepID goes up slightly" intent violated); psychological friction higher.
  - **Failure direction when subtly wrong**: Fails **closed** (user sees no progress even after correct actions; easy to audit because no rows are written). A subtle error (leaking a write) is detectable in `repid_score_events` (append-only audit) or by the `earns_repid_rewards=false` guard.

**(B) is strongly preferred**. It fails closed/safe; the counterparty gate's asymmetry (no protection below ESTABLISHED) makes (A) too easy to abuse at scale. The measured gap (`/builder/token-signup` credential vs. `stake-authorization.ts` full-JWT requirement) shows how easy it is for a "provisional" path to become real.
